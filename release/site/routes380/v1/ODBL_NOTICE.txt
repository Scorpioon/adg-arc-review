The downloadable dataset provides the complete collection of 380 directed 2D walking-route GeoJSON files and a manifest of per-route SHA256, recorded walking length, time and source lineage. The route data are made available under ODbL 1.0, including the share-alike conditions applicable to a derived database. This data-only notice does not apply ODbL to the website's software, editorial texts, photographs or visual design.

Provenance: 323 routes are tied to the official E0_N40.rd5 snapshot of 2026-11-03T01:03:01Z; 57 routes preserve the old_rd5_unverified_vintage lineage. The exact date of the underlying raw OpenStreetMap PBF extract is UNKNOWN and is not inferred from the .rd5 snapshot. BRouter documents CGIAR/CIAT SRTM elevation sources used in segment generation — https://github.com/abrensch/brouter/blob/master/docs/developers/build_segments.md ; source acknowledgement: CGIAR-CSI / CIAT SRTM elevation database — https://bigdata.cgiar.org/srtm-90m-digital-elevation-database/ . No terrain DEM, elevations, original .rd5, WayTags/NodeTags, diagnostics or original router exports are redistributed with this dataset. This acknowledgement is not a claim of separate permission to redistribute any underlying DEM.

The manifest's status value preserves the sanitizer's historical candidate-generation stage. The later publisher rights and geometry approval is documented separately; it supersedes the historical generation status for publication purposes, without changing the manifest or any route SHA256. A remote release is only complete once every one of the 380 files is uploaded and verified by SHA256 before the new application entrypoint is activated.
