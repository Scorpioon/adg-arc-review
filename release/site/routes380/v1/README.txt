ADG-ARC Route380 — 380 directed walking-route GeoJSON dataset

Publisher: ADG-ARC. Publication rights and route-data acceptance approved on 2026-11-04. Prepared for controlled public release; deployment and HTTPS verification are separate stages.

The routes/ directory contains exactly 380 directed 2D GeoJSON FeatureCollections, one for each ordered pair of distinct buildings among 20 (20 x 19). Reverse directions are independent; no self-routes or inferred straight-line fallbacks. manifest.json binds every route ID and path to its own published SHA256, routed walking length, time and source vintage. ODBL_NOTICE.txt contains the license, attribution, data-only scope and the upstream provenance caveats. The accompanying ZIP contains this manifest, the notice, this README and all 380 geometries.

Lineage: 323 official-rd5-snapshot routes and 57 older-vintage routes. The raw OSM PBF extract date is UNKNOWN. The full route collection was accepted by the project publisher; provenance limitations remain documented above.

The manifest records the immutable sanitizer-generation candidate status. The later publication rights approval is described in the notice and publication records, without rewriting the verified route registry or manifest. The website, its software, photographs and editorial content are not automatically licensed under ODbL by the route-data notice.

Deployment prerequisite: the new index.html must not be activated until all 380 remote route files have been independently re-read and verified against their published SHA256. No partial route dataset release.
